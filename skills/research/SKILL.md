# Research

## Purpose & scope
Time-boxed, evidence-based comparison ending in a recommendation.

## Workflow
1. State the decision and criteria (must-haves versus nice-to-haves).
2. Shortlist about 5 candidates at most.
3. Evaluate each: license, release cadence, security advisories, docs quality, production evidence, dependencies, operational burden, lock-in, migration cost.
4. Stars and hype are not evidence. Prefer release notes, advisories, issue health and real adopters.
5. Label each claim **verified** (with source link) or **assumption**.
6. Present a compact comparison table, then one recommendation with the deciding reason and a fallback.
7. Suggest a small proof-of-concept to confirm the choice when stakes are high.

## Evidence required
Source link for every verified claim; assumptions listed separately; date checked for volatile facts.
